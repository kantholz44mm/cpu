\subsection{Educational ISAs}

Commercial designs must consider factors such as size and cost of potential implementations, as well as their performance. For educational purposes, an \ac{isa} may omit features that benefit these goals in order to reduce complexity. By reducing complexity, the fundamental aspects of computation can be highlighted. In addition to that, hardware implementations may omit features that are ubiquitous in modern \acp{cpu} such as branch prediction or caching. There are several \acp{isa} that are designed with their educational value as the primary objective. In this chapter, some of them are explored in order to see where complexity is required and where it can be omitted with a custom design.
