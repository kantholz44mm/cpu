\subsection{PCB}

The \ac{cpu} is implemented as a set of interconnected \acp{pcb}. This approach was mainly chosen due to the high reliability of \ac{pcb} manufacturing. In recent years, the cost of custom \acp{pcb} has dropped significantly, to the point where the pricing is even accessible to hobbyists with very ordered quantities. This approach also has the benfit of offering automated assembly via pick-and-place machines. Many budget \ac{pcb} manufacturers offer both \ac{pcb} production and assembly as a service, resulting in cheap components and quick turn-around times. In addition to these factors, the fully digital design process makes it easier to review changes to the design over time, analyze circuitry before assembly and make incremental changes to parts of a circuit.
