\subsection{Execution model}

The execution model of choice for this project is the register machine. Its working principles are explained in more detail in \autoref{sec:fundamentals_isa_execution_model}. Most modern machines are based on this architecture as well, due to better scalability and flexibility, as well as compilers being easier to optimize for such systems. Since the result of this work shall be used for demonstrating the working principles of digital computers, it makes sense to implement something close to real world systems. More specifically, the architecture of choice obeys the ``Load-store'' principle used in many modern RISC processors.
